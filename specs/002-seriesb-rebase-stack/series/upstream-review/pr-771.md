## What the PR changes
- Its first four commits are #766 (Apple Silicon macOS), which upstream has since merged as `65e46ed6e`, in the stack's base. `git cherry HEAD refs/review/pr771 7d4409054` finds two of them patch-equivalent; the others are in the base in merged form.
- A renderer layer, `src/Core/Renderer/` (28 new files, mostly C: `OOImmediateMode`, `OORenderState(Machine)`, `OORenderPass`, `OOVertexArray`, `OOFixedFunction`, `OORendererGL.c`, `OORendererSDLGPU.c`, `OOGLSLTranslator.c` (glslang + SPIRV-Cross), `OOHLSLCompiler.c` (D3DCompile); three `.m`: `OOGLStateCompat`, `OORendererLog`, `OORendererTexture`).
- 82 existing `src` files changed (+2485/−2020): every immediate-mode draw (entities, HUD, GUI, sky, planets, dust, trumbles, crosshairs, debug drawing) goes through `OOImmediate`/renderer buffers; GL state is mirrored through `OOGL*` wrappers; shader programs, uniforms, textures and post-processing go through the renderer; HDR output; EXR snapshots on every platform.
- `-Drenderer=gl|sdl_gpu` (default `gl`); CI builds `sdl_gpu` on Linux, Windows and macOS; `tools/sdl_gpu_spike` (40 files, the harness and `game-snapshot.py --scenario/--gallery`).
- Open upstream questions: no Windows GPU testing, new glslang/SPIRV-Cross dependencies, and SPIRV-Cross's Apache-2.0 licence, which would make SDL_GPU binaries GPLv3.

## Findings
1. Against HEAD: `git merge-tree --write-tree --merge-base=7d4409054 HEAD refs/review/pr771` gives 91 conflicted files (58 modify/delete for `.m` files that are `.cpp` at HEAD, 28 content, 5 add/add). Over a third of the touched files no longer exist at their upstream paths.
2. A port is a re-implementation, not a merge. The renderer's 24 new C sources and headers (11 `.c`, 13 `.h`) could come in as they are, but the fork compiles its remaining C as C++ (step 021), so it has to decide whether they stay C, given rule 7 (plain and vendored C stay C unless the task is to change them). Its three new `.m` files must be written in C++ on `oofnd`. The 82 call-site edits must be redone by hand in the converted `.cpp` classes, on top of the fork's Google-style layout. The CI jobs must be fitted into the fork's `build-all.yaml`.
3. The PR is still a moving target: it is opt-in, and its licensing and Windows testing are unresolved. It was built on #765, before #766 and everything since, so upstream will need to rebase it first.

## Why this verdict
A large, opt-in, still-unsettled renderer PR that the stack does not carry, as expected. Nothing at HEAD conflicts with its goals, but it cannot be merged mechanically onto the converted tree; porting it is a project of its own.
