Mining Summary.md

What would make a solid mining career story:

- Modern UI
    - EVE-style overview for finding different asteroids in the belt?
	- Ability to look left/right to search for asteroids
	- Target with mining laser, laser points not perfectly straight, but can mine nearby asteroids.
	- Map: Find asteroid belts, station on map.
	- https://wiki.alioth.net/index.php/System_Map
	- Click to select different menus, maybe Esc for pause menu.
	- See cargo hold filling up while mining.
- Navigation controls. Maybe more like Everspace where you can more easily look left and right and click to fire.
	- There is a controls list here, which could serve as reference. https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=478686362
- Different mining ships, mining lasers, ore types, ore hold...
- Sell your ore to different stations and places.
- Industrial story with manufacturing, refining, blueprints for rockets, ships, etc.
