## Notes

- **Not worth chasing:** `legal/copyright`, `build/c++17` and `readability/todo` are Google
  house rules. `whitespace/tab`, `whitespace/line_length` and `whitespace/braces` in the
  `clang-format off` tables are deliberate. A `CPPLINT.cfg` that sets `linelength=80` and filters
  `-legal/copyright,-build/c++17,-readability/todo`, plus excluding the table headers, would leave
  about 2,700 findings.
- **Worth a task:** `build/include_what_you_use` (536) makes headers self-contained.
  `runtime/explicit` (23) and `readability/casting` (14) are small and real. `runtime/int` (354)
  overlaps `specs/009-modern-cpp-std`, so check that spec before taking it.
- **Clang-format would fix:** most of the remaining whitespace and brace findings outside the
  tables, if it were run over those files. The rule is to run it only on files you change.
