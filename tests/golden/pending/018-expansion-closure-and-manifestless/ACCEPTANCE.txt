WHAT IS NOT HERE, AND WHY
-------------------------
No line launches the game. A live run takes ~15s and two game processes, and a stored acceptance
line that launches Oolite fails for reasons that are not findings: an orphaned oolite.exe from an
unrelated run holds console port 8563 and turns a correct tree red. The live evidence is recorded
in provenance.json (10 runs, 10 DUMPED, byte-identical) and the two live mutant arms are recorded
there too, with their measured rc, wall time and message. The live re-run procedure is in
LANDING.md for whoever lands the golden.
